## Suggested Image Style

If real photographs are unavailable, use high-quality royalty-free images representing:

- Volunteers teaching children
- Food distribution
- Tree plantation
- Beach cleanup
- Blood donation
- Community service
- Group volunteering
- Outreach activities

### Image Style Guidelines
- Natural
- Warm
- Documentary-like
- Hopeful
- Authentic
- Diverse

### Avoid
- Corporate office photos
- Overly staged stock photos
- Busy collages
- Artificial-looking AI faces
- Dark, gloomy imagery
- Generic abstract visuals where real community photos would be better
