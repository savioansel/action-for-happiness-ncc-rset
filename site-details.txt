1. OVERVIEW & GOAL
------------------
Name: Action for Happiness (Community Outreach Initiative)
Affiliation: An Initiative of the NCC Unit, Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology (RSET)

Goal / Core Purpose:
To connect students, professionals, and members of the public with meaningful opportunities to serve communities through education, healthcare, environmental protection, and humanitarian outreach.
