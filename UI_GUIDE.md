# UI_GUIDE.md

## Purpose

This document defines the visual language for the Community Outreach Initiative website.

The goal is to create a premium, trustworthy, modern nonprofit website suitable for presentation to the Director of Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology (Autonomous).

The overall feeling should be inspired by Action for Happiness—not copied, but similar in clarity, simplicity, and storytelling.

---

# Design Keywords

- Modern
- Premium
- Warm
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Community-driven
- Human-centered
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Hopeful

Avoid:
- Generic templates
- Bootstrap appearance
- Overly bright colors
- Excessive gradients
- Clutter
- Stock corporate style

---

# Layout

The website should be a **single-page landing page** with smooth scrolling.

Section order:

1. Header
2. Hero
3. About
4. Mission
5. Service Areas
6. How It Works
7. Upcoming Activities
8. Volunteer Registration
9. Impact
10. Testimonials
11. FAQ
12. Contact
13. Footer

Each section should have generous spacing (100–140px vertical).

Alternate subtle background colors between sections.

---

# Visual Style

Background:
- Primary: White (#FFFFFF)
- Secondary: Warm Cream (#FAF8F4)

Accent Colors:
- Navy: #1E2A44
- Orange: #F26B4A
- Green: #5F8D4E

Cards:
- Rounded corners (20–24px)
- Soft shadows
- Slight hover lift
- Clean spacing

Buttons:
Primary
- Navy background
- White text
- Rounded full radius

Secondary
- White background
- Navy border
- Hover fill

---

# Typography

Body:
- Inter

Headings:
- Playfair Display

Heading hierarchy:
- Hero: 56–72px desktop
- Section: 36–48px
- Card Titles: 20–24px
- Body: 16–18px

Keep paragraphs short.

---

# Hero

Large full-screen hero.

Use an inspiring community-service image.

Large headline.

Short subtitle.

Two CTA buttons.

Include subtle floating shapes or gradient glow.

---

# Navigation

Sticky navigation.

Transparent initially.

Solid background after scrolling.

Include:
- NCC Logo
- RSET Logo
- Navigation links
- CTA button ("Volunteer")

Mobile:
Hamburger menu with smooth animation.

---

# Animations

Use Framer Motion.

Animation style:
- Fade up
- Fade in
- Small scale
- Gentle stagger

Avoid:
- Bounce
- Flash
- Spin
- Dramatic parallax

Animations should feel calm.

---

# Cards

Mission cards

Service cards

Activity cards

Should all share one consistent design language.

Hover:
- Slight elevation
- Shadow increase
- Tiny scale (1.02 max)

---

# Impact Section

Large animated counters.

Example:
520+
Volunteers

Use subtle count-up animation.

---

# Forms

Simple.

Large inputs.

Rounded borders.

Clear validation.

No unnecessary fields.

---

# Icons

Use Lucide React.

Maintain one consistent stroke width.

---

# Images

Use authentic volunteer/community images.

Avoid:
- Artificial AI-looking people
- Corporate office photography
- Busy collages

Prefer:
- Volunteers teaching children
- Tree plantation
- Food distribution
- Blood donation
- Community cleanup

---

# Accessibility

- Good color contrast
- Keyboard accessible
- Semantic HTML
- Visible focus states
- Responsive typography

---

# Mobile

Design mobile-first.

Target width:
375px+

Spacing should remain generous.

Cards should stack vertically.

Buttons should become full-width where appropriate.

---

# Performance

Optimize all images.

Lazy load below-the-fold images.

Use Next.js Image component.

Minimize unnecessary client-side JavaScript.

---

# Overall Impression

The website should look like it was designed by a professional UI/UX agency for a national nonprofit organization.

A visitor should immediately feel:

"This initiative is trustworthy, professionally managed, welcoming, and making a real impact."
